Per-Residue Immunogenicity Risk Classification of Manufacturing Impurities in Semaglutide: Substitution, Deletion, and Insertion Sensitivity Profiles

A systematic computational analysis of all 31 residue positions in semaglutide, evaluating how three classes of manufacturing impurities — amino acid substitutions, deletions, and insertions — alter immunogenicity risk. Each position is scored for its impact on immune recognition, identifying critical hotspots where manufacturing impurities carry elevated clinical concern.
